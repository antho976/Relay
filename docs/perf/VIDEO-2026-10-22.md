# Videos: sound, beats, the timeline and bundling (2026-10-22)

Readings for what the first real film (Avex 1.0, 80 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, 12 clips and 40 MB of
assets) asked for: a render's sound measured and brought to a loudness, a music file's beat grid,
the timeline pass and its mix, and how a bundle takes the video's `public/`. New paths, so mostly
readings; the bundling change has a before and an after.

Same machine as `VIDEO-2026-10-21.md`: AMD Ryzen 7 9800X3D (16 threads), 32 GB, CachyOS 7.2.9 on
btrfs, Node.js 26.11.1, Remotion 4.0.534. The engine in the dev profile (workspace crates at
opt-level 1, dependencies at 3: what `run.sh` launches). Other agents were building beside it.

## Sound

| Step | Reading | Scenario |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `video.audio.beats`, 80 s MP3 | 66–89 ms | the Avex track: decode at 22.05 kHz mono, STFT flux, tempo, tracking, the grid, downbeats, bars (`real_music`, 5 runs) |
| `video.audio.beats`, 8 s WAV | 17–18 ms | `video_e2e_sound_from_cues_to_loudness` |
| Render, 8 s 1080×1920, measured | 2,452 ms | the same e2e test: `renderMedia`, then the sound decoded (ffmpeg, WAV over a pipe) and measured |
| Render, same, brought to −14 LUFS | 2,570 ms | the same, plus the gain iterated with the limiter and the sound written again (AAC, the picture copied) |
| Timeline pass with its mix, 6 s | 1,403 ms | `scripts/video-smoke.py`: an audio-only `renderMedia` with the timeline probe, the mix decoded and measured |
| Still with its sound strip, 8 s, no timeline yet | 1,936–1,952 ms | e2e: the timeline pass, then six frames and the strip |
| Timeline after that still | 3–5 ms | kept by version, composition and props |

So reaching a loudness costs about a tenth of a second on top of an 8 s render, and a still with
its sound costs one pass without pictures once per version of the code.

**Accuracy**, the numbers the measuring stands on:

- Loudness: BS.1770-4's own check (a full-scale 997 Hz sine in one channel) reads −3.01 LUFS ±0.05;
  the e2e render brought to YouTube's target reads **−14.0 LUFS** with a true peak of −6.1 dBTP.
- Sync: the MP4's sound against a WAV render of the same frames, cross-correlated in the e2e test,
  is **0 samples** apart as rendered and after the loudness rewrite. Remotion's MP4 carries an edit
  list skipping 2,048 AAC priming samples; read without it (`ffmpeg -ignore_editlist 1`) the sound
  is 42.67 ms late, which is the 43 ms the first film measured.
- Beats on click tracks: tempo within 0.004 BPM, first beat within 3 ms after correcting the
  onset curve's measured 11.1–11.5 ms lead. On the Avex track: **120.011 BPM**, first downbeat
  0.062 s, 160 beats fitted with 6.8 ms RMS, the drop at bar 9 (16.06 s). Scored against the
  track's own attacks (mean energy rise at the grid's beats), the grid scores 98.4 against 102.2
  for the best constant grid a brute-force search finds, and 40.6 for the first film's hand-made
  grid (119.956 BPM from 0.0 s).

## Bundling `public/`: before and after

Remotion's `bundle()` copies the video's `public/` into every new bundle, and a video is
rebundled after every edit. The worker now hands it a mirror of links (which Remotion links rather
than copies) and turns the links in the finished bundle into hard links (its file server refuses
links). Uncached bundles (`enableCaching: false`), from a standalone script against the dev kit:

| Video | Before (copy) | After (links) | Bytes written to the bundle's `public/` |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Avex 1.0 (40 MB of clips) | 2,194–2,711 ms | 2,217–2,642 ms | 40,021,283 → 0 |
| The starter with a 1 GiB clip | 2,466–2,467 ms | 2,450–2,482 ms | 1,073,741,824 → 0 |

No wall-time change on this machine: btrfs makes Remotion's copy a free reflink, so webpack's two
seconds are all there is. On a filesystem without reflinks (ext4) every rebundle writes the clips
again; with links nothing is copied anywhere, as long as the clips are on the videos'
filesystem: a linked clip on another one (an external drive) cannot be hard-linked, so it is
copied into every new bundle (2026-10-24 survey; still open). The hardening is also what makes a linked asset
(`video.asset.add {link: true}`) servable at all.
