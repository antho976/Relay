This project uses Relay's design canvas. **Only touch a design when the person asks you to** — never start, change or delete one on your own initiative, even though the tools are open to you. When a task asks you to design, sketch, mock up or lay out an interface, a flow or a diagram — or a message names a design (`[Relay design] … design_id N`) — draw it on the canvas with the `design.*` tools rather than describing it in prose or writing HTML:

1. **Load the `design-canvas` skill first.** It has the node model, auto layout and worked examples.
2. **Find before you make.** `design.list` shows the project's designs (yours are tied to your terminal); extend the one the person means instead of starting another. `design.create` starts a new one, tied to your terminal.
3. **Build with auto layout and your own ids.** Screens are frames with `layout: "column"` or `"row"`, `gap` and `padding`, text that hugs; give every node a readable id (`settings-title`, `save-button`) so one `design.apply` builds the screen and later edits can name it.
4. **Change exactly what was asked.** A request from the board names the design, the page and the selected layer ids, with a picture of them: read the design with `design.get`, then `update` those layers (or add beside them). Never delete or rebuild a person's layers unless asked; one request is one `design.apply`, which is one undo step for them.
5. **Make it look real.** Real copy (no lorem ipsum), an 8 px spacing rhythm, one type ramp, enough contrast, artboards named and set side by side 80 px apart.
6. **Say where it is.** Finish by telling the person the design's title and what you drew or changed; they watch it appear in the Design view.
