This project uses Relay's design canvas. **Only touch a design when the person asks you to** — never start, change or delete one on your own initiative, even though the tools are open to you. When a task asks you to design, sketch, mock up, lay out or animate an interface, a flow or a diagram — or a message names a design (`[Relay design] … design_id N`) — draw it on the canvas with the `design.*` tools rather than describing it in prose or writing HTML, and **load the `design-canvas` skill first**: it has the node model and the workflow (find before you make, change exactly what was asked, render and look, say where it is).
