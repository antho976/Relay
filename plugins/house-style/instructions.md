This project has the House style: the owner's design taste and the fundamentals under it. It applies to every interface you build or change here.

- **Read the owner's list first.** `my-list.md` in the `house-style` skill folder holds the rules they keep by hand. Only what they say in the conversation beats it; it wins over everything else here and over the project's design doc. Never edit it unless they ask: when they correct a design, offer the new line for them to add.
- **Load `house-style` before any UI work**: a screen, a component, a mockup, a design-canvas artboard, a redesign, a polish pass or a critique. Load `house-style-native` for the stack you are on (GTK 4, Android Compose, web, design canvas, game UI) and run `house-style-review` before you call UI work done.
- **Precedence:** what the owner says now, then `my-list.md`, then the project's own design doc (`DESIGN.md`, `.claude/DESIGN.md`, `PRODUCT.md` and their removals and decisions ledgers), then the house style, then generic design skills such as impeccable. `house-style` also routes motion, unslop and polish passes, worst-case data, mobile web, library picks and variations to its own references, which replace the separate design skills they were merged from. Where a generic skill pushes for bold or expressive treatment of a working screen, the house style wins.
- **The look:** clean and professional, near-black with one accent for what asks for you, colour in data, trim controls, moderate corners, no boxes around passive content, no decorative lines, nothing crowded, oversized, dated or AI-looking, and one look across all the owner's apps.
- **Change exactly what was asked.** "The rest is fine" means leave the rest. Keep what the owner likes: the idea, a liked old version, the fonts. In Relay, never restyle the terminal panes unless asked.
- **For a redesign, make one version beside the before** unless they ask for options (then 2–3 that really differ), one screen at a time, main page first. On a Relay project mockups go on the design canvas.
- **Look before you report.** Render or screenshot the result at real size and fix what you see first. A web page is shown in a browser on localhost, checked at every width. If you could not render it, say so.
- **Write down a decision the owner makes or reverses** in the project's design doc the same turn.
